### Concept Overview

Thousands of dark grains flow across a parchment-toned paper canvas, assembling into line-drawn symbols of timekeeping before dissolving into windblown gusts toward the right edge. Underneath each motif, delicate serif typography states the era and mechanism.

### Visual Identity
- **Color Palette:** Warm antique cream background (`#ECE5D3` to `#E3D8BE` radial vignette) with dark charcoal/brown umber sand granules (`#2A241F`).
- **Typography:** Refined, classic serif typeface (e.g., Garamond, Cormorant Garamond, or Baskerville) set in lowercase small-caps for dates and descriptions.
- **Particle Aesthetic:** Dense clustering on solid fills, feathered organic edges, and subtle continuous motion (trickling sand, ticking hands, oscillating pendulums).

### Timed Scene Progression
1. **00:00–08.00 — The Sun (c. 3500 BCE):** Grains pool at the base and stream upward to form a radiating obelisk/sun shadow.
2. **08.00–17.00 — Water (c. 1500 BCE):** Grains sweep in to construct a bowl dripping measured drops into a reservoir.
3. **17.00–26.00 — Sand (c. 1300):** Hourglass silhouette forms; dynamic interior stream flows down into the lower bulb.
4. **26.00–35.00 — The Pendulum (1656):** Grandfather clock case forms; internal pendulum sways continuously.
5. **35.00–44.00 — The Pocket (1700s):** Pocket watch with top loop and winding chain; minute hand ticks.
6. **44.00–53.00 — The Railway (1883):** Steam locomotive and station clock establish standardized railway time.

8. **62.00–71.00 — The Pocket, Again (Today):** Modern smartphone bezel with digital time display (`9:41`) and notification cards.
9. **71.00–86.00 — Concluding Hourglass & Title:** Hourglass reappears while sand trickles through to fade out with final title 'time, told in sand'.
